Movie images all must be JPG/JPEG|PNG.

Movie sounds must be AU|MP3 sound format for MOV;
must be WAV sound format for AVI.

A good speech dict site: freetranslations.org

You can convert to AU with this command:
/home/muratsivas76/istasyon/java/ffmpeg/bin/ffmpeg.exe -y -vn -i file.mp3 -acodec pcm_mulaw -ac 1 -ar 8000 -ab 64k -f au file.au

You can convert to WAV with this command:
/home/muratsivas76/istasyon/java/ffmpeg/bin/ffmpeg.exe -i file.mp3 -c:a pcm_s16le -ar 11025 -ac 2 -y file.wav

You can restore your MP3 file with this command:
/home/muratsivas76/istasyon/java/ffmpeg/bin/ffmpeg.exe -i srcFile.mp3 -ar 24000 -ac 2 -ab 128K -vn -y dstFile.mp3

You can convert from MOV to AVI with this command:
/home/muratsivas76/istasyon/java/ffmpeg/bin/ffmpeg.exe -i file.mov -q:v 1 -c:v mjpeg -ar 44100 -ac 2 -y file.avi

You can convert from MOV to Facebook accepted MP4 with this command:
/home/muratsivas76/istasyon/java/ffmpeg/bin/ffmpeg.exe -i file.mov -c:v libx264 -crf 23 -pix_fmt yuv420p -y file.mp4

You can learn MP3 durations of a directory with this command:
/home/muratsivas76/istasyon/java/ffmpeg/bin/ffprobe.exe -v error -show_entries format=duration -of default=noprint_wrappers=1:nokey=1 -sexagesimal file.mp3

You can increment the volume of a file with this command:
/home/muratsivas76/istasyon/java/ffmpeg/bin/ffmpeg.exe -i file.mp3 -af volume=10dB aumVolumFile.mp3
